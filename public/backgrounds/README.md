# Generated landscape assets

These seventeen local WebP images were generated with OpenAI image generation for
SuperChrome on 2026-09-18. The first ten photographic scenes are 1672 × 941
WebP images at quality 82. The seven abstract scenes were exported at
3344 × 1882 with Lanczos resampling and WebP quality 95 to reduce visible
compression and scaling artifacts on high-density displays. They have no
external runtime dependency.

- `mountain-river.webp`: cinematic dark mountain valley and winding river,
  based on the concept image chosen during design review.
- `misty-coast.webp`: slate-blue cliffs and sea at dawn.
- `desert-light.webp`: warm dunes and distant rock ridges at golden hour.
- `forest-dusk.webp`: evergreen valley and river in blue dusk mist.
- `moonlit-lake.webp`: moonlit lake and turquoise shore mist.
- `enchanted-forest.webp`: ancient woodland with fog and subtle fireflies.
- `alpine-dawn.webp`: snowy peaks and glacial lake at first light.
- `volcanic-coast.webp`: black cliffs and ocean at blue hour.
- `rainy-city.webp`: modern skyline and wet streets at dusk.
- `riverside-city.webp`: historic stone bridges and warm lights at night.
- `liquid-ribbons.webp`: original black, gold and violet liquid edge study.
- `peach-gel.webp`: original glossy coral and amber gel forms.
- `pastel-blobs.webp`: original floating blue, pink and violet forms.
- `silk-plume.webp`: original flowing color and silk-like folds.
- `light-columns.webp`: original orange and blue translucent vertical light.
- `color-marbling.webp`: original iridescent blue and lime marbling.
- `neon-liquid.webp`: original deep blue, cyan and violet liquid forms.

The first ten prompts requested wide photographic scenes. The last seven used
user-provided images as style references for new abstract compositions. All
prompts requested a clear area for the search palette and no text, logos or
watermarks. Motion is added in code.

`charcoal-folds.svg` and `dark-facets.svg` are original, resolution-independent
minimal wallpapers based on the two later references. They use a few large
near-black shapes and a very slow CSS drift, without glow or echo layers.

`woven-sand.svg`, `woven-sage.svg`, and `woven-blue.svg` are three colorways of
one original brush-line wallpaper inspired by the warm pattern reference. They
use the same vector paths at 3840 × 2160 and the minimal backdrop's slow drift.

`chevron-gold.svg` and `chevron-ink.svg` are repeating line chevrons;
`interlock-blue.svg` and `interlock-olive.svg` use broad interlocking ribbon
shapes with fine vector hatching. All are self-contained SVG wallpapers.
